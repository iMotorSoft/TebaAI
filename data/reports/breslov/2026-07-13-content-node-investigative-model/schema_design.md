`ContentUnit` is the acyclic tree. `ContentNode` is the local, searchable,
citable piece and is primary or satellite. A citable node has a `PageAnchor`.
`LiteralSpan` proves a claim; `SemanticUnit` derives retrieval only inside its
node. `ContentRelation` records typed evidence and authority, never promoting
same-page cross-role context to a strong relation. `library_investigative_evidence_view`
provides citation-ready fields.

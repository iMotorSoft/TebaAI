# Accessibility results

- Semantic `header`, `main`, `section`, `nav`, `aside`, `footer`, heading hierarchy, descriptive landscape alt, and decorative `aria-hidden` marks.
- Keyboard-visible focus, 44px mobile menu target, native buttons, `aria-expanded`, labelled language selector, and labelled mobile navigation.
- Hebrew identity is marked `lang="he" dir="rtl"`.
- Reduced-motion media query removes motion and smooth scrolling.
- Navy/ivory primary text and CTA treatments use high-contrast combinations. The smallest capability copy was kept at a readable mobile scale rather than hidden.

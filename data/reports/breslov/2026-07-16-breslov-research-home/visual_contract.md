# Visual contract

- Navy, warm ivory, parchment, muted gold and ink palette implemented in shared CSS tokens.
- Exact visible product title: `BRESLOV RESEARCH`; Rebe identity uses `רבי נחמן` with `lang="he" dir="rtl"` and `REBE NAJMÁN`.
- BRI is a secondary collaboration card with a generic building symbol; no BRI logo is asserted or used.
- The approved quote and source are rendered over the desktop landscape; the quote is intentionally omitted on narrow mobile to protect readability.
- Desktop uses a two-column editorial hero and five capability columns. Tablet becomes 3 + 2. Mobile uses 3 + 2, falling back to 2 + 2 + 1 below 360px.
- The main landscape is local, responsive WebP (`768`, `1200`, `1600`) with explicit dimensions, `srcset`, `sizes`, eager priority, and no external request.

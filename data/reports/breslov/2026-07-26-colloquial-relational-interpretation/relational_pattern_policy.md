# Relational pattern policy

Open relational understanding uses small ordered ES, EN, and HE pattern groups. Each pattern has an auditable identifier, an instruction capture, and a subject capture.

Supported forms include instruction-first and subject-first order, relationship nouns, association phrases, “with what” questions, and the equivalent English and Hebrew constructions.

One explicit subject plus an open relationship request resolves to `concept_cooccurrence`. Two grounded subjects plus a binary marker resolve to `relation_query`. A bare conjunction does not create a relation by itself.

Markup, control characters, and bidi override controls invalidate relational subject extraction.

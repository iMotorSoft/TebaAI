# V2 audit reasons

- `ZONE_AMBIGUOUS`: composed editorial note-zone proof absent; extractor label alone never sufficient.
- `DIRECTION_UNPROVEN`: missing pure supported script or visible horizontal native token-order evidence.
- `PROVENANCE_OVERLAP`: missing/duplicate lineage or conflicting source-line geometry.
- `BOUNDARY_UNPROVEN`: no explicit mapped note start/local boundary.
- `COLUMN_AMBIGUOUS`: non-consecutive markers, invalid column ownership or unsupported flow.
- `CROSS_PAGE_CONTINUATION_UNRESOLVED`: no proven editorial closure; page cuts do not complete notes.
- `LAYOUT_UNSUPPORTED`: non-note family lacks V2 proof collector.
- `CORRUPT_TEXT_SIGNAL`: U+FFFD in source layout, not a proven source-PDF defect.
- `PROVENANCE_UNRESOLVED_QUALITY_UNRESOLVED`: quality blocked by provenance.

After certification only, existing V1 quality reasons may remain, notably `NATIVE_VISIBLE_TEXT_ORDER_EQUIVALENT` and `QUALITY_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`. Each named proof separately exposes passed/missing status and evidence identifiers. No confidence threshold or heuristic sum.

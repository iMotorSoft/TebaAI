Git searches include reachable history across refs, per-pair `-G`, matching historical patches, current consumer diff and reflog. No reachable V5 module/test commit exists. The second pass independently searches terminal/leading source snippets, decodes JSON text, rechecks supporting tests and historical patches, and resolves number collisions. It is a second algorithmic pass by the same agent, not a second reviewer. No new source adjudication was performed.

## Why the non-use chains are unresolved

1. **A / C / D (later first appearance):** the all-pages scan and hybrid positive contracts postdate the current-state cutoff, and code/test hashes subsequently agree. However the relevant source endpoints and several explicit positive cases predate V5. The later contract cannot be substituted for their first attributable project appearance.
2. **B (complete inventory):** the original `development-opened-pages.json` enumerates 32 probes, and all candidates are absent. But the later builder's `v5_manual` additionally declares ranges 54–59, 106–111, 165–170 and 205–208, several pages outside that inventory. Its header describes probes/source review, not a complete independently anchored record of all design/debug/proof cases. Neither list demonstrates exhaustive pre-stabilization development provenance.
3. The builder's comment claiming these pairs were never used is a useful historical assertion, but its generated inventory uses hard-coded sets. The subsequent `resume-lineage-decision.json` explicitly says the audit was INCOMPLETE and proof/threshold tuning NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED. The assertion therefore cannot independently close that gap.
4. The later evaluator-only changes are documented and isolated by hashes. They do not imply V5 use, and no real candidate V5 output was observed. They also cannot establish the missing pre-cutoff history.

This does not demand that every threshold have a causal diary, or that nobody ever saw a page. A complete development inventory or defensible earlier-state/discovery chain would suffice. Neither is established here. No *referenced mandatory existing report* was found missing; the unavailable evidence is the intermediate uncommitted implementation / comprehensive development record needed to distinguish pre-cutoff non-use from unknown influence. It was not fabricated from filesystem times.
